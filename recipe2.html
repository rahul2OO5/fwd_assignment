Grilled Paneer Tikka

⏱ Prep time: 30 Minutes Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 400g Paneer (cubed)
  • 1/2 cup Thick Yogurt
  • 1 tbsp Besan (Gram Flour)
  • 1 tsp Turmeric & Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Chaat Masala
  • 1 large Capsicum (cubed)
  • 1 large Onion (cubed)
  • Lemon juice to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Marinate: Whisk yogurt, besan, and all dry spices in a large bowl until smooth.
  2. Coat: Add the paneer, capsicum, and onions to the marinade. Toss gently to coat well and let it rest for 20 minutes.
  3. Skewers: Thread the marinated paneer and vegetables alternately onto metal or soaked wooden skewers.
  4. Cook: Grill in a preheated oven at 200°C (400°F) or pan-fry on a hot skillet until the edges are charred and golden brown.
  5. Serve: Sprinkle generously with chaat masala and a squeeze of fresh lemon juice before serving hot with mint chutney!

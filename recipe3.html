Homemade Garlic Naan

⏱ Prep time: 20 Minutes Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 cups All-Purpose Flour (Maida)
  • 1/2 tsp Baking Powder
  • 1/4 tsp Baking Soda
  • 1/2 cup Plain Yogurt
  • 3 tbsp Minced Garlic
  • Freshly Chopped Coriander
  • Melted Butter or Ghee
  • Pinch of Salt

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Make Dough: In a bowl, mix flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Add yogurt and knead into a soft dough. Let it rest for 10-15 minutes.
  2. Roll: Divide the dough into small balls and roll them out into flat oval shapes using a rolling pin.
  3. Flavor: Press the minced garlic and fresh coriander firmly onto the top side of the rolled dough.
  4. Water Trick: Brush the *bottom* side (without garlic) with a little bit of water.
  5. Cook: Place the wet side down on a hot iron skillet (tawa). Once bubbles form, flip the skillet upside down directly over the flame to char the top side.
  6. Butter: Remove from the skillet and brush generously with melted butter or ghee while still hot.
